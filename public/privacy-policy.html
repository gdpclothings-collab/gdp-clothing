GDP Clothing

Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026 · Last updated September 18, 2026

GDP Clothing is an independent Canadian apparel and custom-printing business based in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. This Privacy Policy explains how GDP Clothing collects, uses, stores, protects, and shares personal information when you browse the website, create an account, use Google Sign-In, place an order, use Custom Studio or DTF tools, upload artwork, contact support, or otherwise interact with GDP Clothing.

Information we collect

  • Name, email address, telephone number, shipping address, and billing details.
  • Account and authentication information used to create and secure your GDP Clothing account.
  • Order history, order notes, support communications, returns, fulfillment, and customer-service records.
  • Photographs, artwork, design instructions, proofs, production files, and other content you voluntarily upload for custom products.
  • Marketing consent and withdrawal records when applicable.
  • Technical and security information such as device/browser information, IP-related security signals, session information, and operational logs needed for fraud prevention, account security, checkout operation, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

Google Sign-In and Google user data

When you choose Sign in with Google, GDP Clothing may receive basic account information made available by Google, including your name, email address, profile image, and a Google account identifier. GDP Clothing uses this information only to authenticate you, create or connect your GDP Clothing account, maintain account security, and provide account-related services.

GDP Clothing does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, or other Google services beyond the basic profile and identity information required for sign-in.

Google Sign-In information used for authentication may be stored with your GDP Clothing account through Supabase, our authentication and application-data provider. GDP Clothing does not sell Google user data and does not use Google Sign-In data for targeted advertising.

You may revoke GDP Clothing's Google access through your Google Account permissions. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of eligible GDP Clothing account information by contacting support@gdpclothing.ca.

How we use information

GDP Clothing uses personal information to provide and operate the storefront, authenticate accounts, maintain account security, prepare and fulfill orders, produce custom apparel and print work, process payments and refunds, provide customer support, prevent fraud and abuse, troubleshoot and improve service reliability, maintain business and accounting records, comply with legal obligations, and send marketing communications only where permitted and consented to where required.

Payments

GDP Clothing does not intentionally collect or store full payment-card numbers or card security codes in its own application database. Payment-card entry and payment processing are handled through Stripe-controlled payment components and services.

Service providers and sharing

GDP Clothing uses service providers to operate the website and business, including Cloudflare for website delivery and edge security, Supabase for authentication, application data and storage, GitHub for source control and deployment workflows, and Stripe for payment processing. Personal information is shared with these providers only as reasonably necessary to provide their services, secure the platform, fulfill orders, process payments, or meet applicable legal obligations.

GDP Clothing does not sell personal information or Google user data.

Custom artwork and photos

Customer-provided photos, artwork, design files, and instructions are used to create requested previews, proofs, products, and production files. Access is limited to the customer and authorized GDP Clothing personnel or systems that need the files for order fulfillment, support, quality control, or production.

Cookies, sessions, and technical information

GDP Clothing may use cookies, local storage, authentication sessions, and similar technologies that are necessary to keep you signed in, maintain a shopping cart, preserve design work, protect accounts, detect abuse, and operate the website reliably. Where analytics or optional marketing technologies are used, GDP Clothing will apply applicable consent and notice requirements.

Retention

GDP Clothing keeps personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, active customer accounts and orders, required business records, security and fraud prevention, dispute handling, accounting and tax obligations, or other legal requirements. Customer-uploaded design and production files may be retained for a limited period where needed to fulfill, reproduce, support, or resolve issues relating to an order.

Security

GDP Clothing uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including access controls, authentication protections, encrypted connections, platform security controls, and restricted administrative access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but GDP Clothing works to reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.

International processing

Some service providers may process or store information outside Saskatchewan or Canada. When information is processed in another jurisdiction, it may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction. GDP Clothing uses service providers and safeguards appropriate to its operations and the information involved.

Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion review, or information about how your data is handled. A deletion request may not require immediate deletion of records that GDP Clothing must reasonably retain for transactions, tax, accounting, fraud prevention, disputes, security, or legal obligations.

Registered customers may also use available account privacy controls. Requests can be sent to support@gdpclothing.ca.

Marketing communications

Marketing consent is separate from transactional communications such as order confirmations, account notices, payment messages, and support responses. Where marketing consent is required, it is optional and can be withdrawn.

Children's privacy

GDP Clothing's services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact GDP Clothing so the matter can be reviewed.

Privacy incidents

If a privacy or security incident creates a reporting, recordkeeping, or notification obligation under applicable law, GDP Clothing will assess the incident and follow the required process.

Changes to this policy

GDP Clothing may update this Privacy Policy when business practices, services, legal requirements, or technology change. The latest version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective or revision date.

Contact

Privacy questions, Google Sign-In questions, or privacy requests may be submitted to support@gdpclothing.ca.
